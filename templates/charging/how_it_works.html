Charging and payments

Scan. Pay. Charge.

A simple charging journey from finding a connector to authorizing your session.

How to charge

Four steps at the station.

01

Choose a station

Use the network locator to find a site, review connector information and check the latest recorded charger status.

02

Scan the QR code

Scan the code attached to your charger. It opens the secure session page for that individual unit.

03

Select and pay

Choose a prepaid amount and a payment method, such as mobile money or card. A provider must confirm payment before any authorization request.

04

Start charging

After verified payment, the station controller must accept the request. The session begins only after the charger confirms authorization.

Payments

Clear payment status before a session begins.

Planned payment methods include mobile money and bank cards through a secure payment provider. NEXT GEN POWER will not collect or store raw card details.

Payment processing and charger authorization are currently integration points. Until payment and charger-controller adapters are configured and tested for the operating network, no live payment will be taken and no charging session will be authorized through this demonstration site.

Published charger availability must come from the live station management system. Demonstration statuses on this site are clearly marked and should not be used to plan a real trip.
