About NEXT GEN POWER

Better journeys start with better access.

We connect people and businesses in Ghana with trusted electric vehicles and the charging infrastructure to support them.

Our role

Making electric mobility easier to choose and use.

NEXT GEN POWER is a Ghanaian electric vehicle dealership and charging infrastructure company. We source vehicles from established manufacturers rather than building our own cars.

Alongside vehicle sales, we own and operate charging stations, with charger status, QR-based session entry and digital payment designed as part of one customer journey.

Our work is practical: present accurate vehicle information, make charging easier to find and build operations that can grow reliably.

OUR MISSION

Make electric mobility practical.

Connect Ghanaian drivers and businesses with trusted EV choices, useful charging access and clear support through the ownership journey.

OUR VISION

Power a connected transport future.

Help grow an accessible, dependable EV ecosystem in Ghana, supported by well-managed charging infrastructure.

OUR BUSINESS MODEL

Retail and infrastructure.

Source and retail vehicles from established manufacturers, then build recurring charging services through owned and operated network sites.
