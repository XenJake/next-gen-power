Long-term strategy

Cleaner energy, built into the network.

Our charging infrastructure is being designed to support a more resilient, lower-carbon energy system as the network grows.

Energy approach

Charging infrastructure can do more.

Solar generation and battery energy storage are part of NEXT GEN POWER's long-term station strategy. Together, they can help reduce reliance on grid power, manage peak demand and improve resilience.

Specific deployments, capacity figures and emissions claims will be published only when measured and confirmed for an operating site.

Electric mobility

A considered step toward cleaner transport.

Electric vehicles have no tailpipe exhaust while driving, which can improve roadside air quality where they replace combustion-engine trips. Their full climate impact depends on electricity generation, vehicle production, battery supply chains and how long the vehicle is used.

We will avoid one-size-fits-all emissions or savings claims. As our charging network grows, we intend to report energy sources, solar generation, storage performance and site-level progress using measured data.
